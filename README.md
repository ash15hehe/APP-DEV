# Stranger Things

## About the Show
**Stranger Things** is a thrilling sci-fi horror series set in the 1980s in the fictional town of **Hawkins, Indiana**. It follows a group of kids who uncover a web of government secrets, supernatural forces, and a terrifying alternate dimension known as **The Upside Down**.

---

##  Main Characters
* **Eleven (Jane Hopper)** - A girl with psychokinetic and telepathic abilities.
* **Mike Wheeler** - The logical and loyal leader of the group.
* **Dustin Henderson** - The science-loving, lovable strategist.
* **Lucas Sinclair** - The pragmatic and level-headed member.
* **Will Byers** - The boy whose mysterious disappearance kicks off the story.
* **Max Mayfield** - The fierce and independent skater girl.

---

##  Iconic Season Highlights
* **Season 1:** The disappearance of Will Byers and the arrival of El.
* **Season 2:** The Shadow Monster and the Mind Flayer.
* **Season 3:** The secret Russian base underneath Starcourt Mall.
* **Season 4:** Vecna's curse and the ultimate battle for Hawkins.
